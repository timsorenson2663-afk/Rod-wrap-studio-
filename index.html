📖 Rod Wrap Terminology & Pattern Glossary

CROSS Geometry

Passes where threads travel diagonally from butt to tip across opposing layout axes, crossing at 0°/180° (or 90°/270°) intersections to form traditional diamonds or cross motifs.

BOX Geometry

Sequences where thread passes frame a diamond center or Maltese cross, creating closed square outer borders that isolate patterns and transition into background fill.

CHEVRON Geometry

Unidirectional arrow or V-shape wrapping passes pointing toward the butt or tip of the rod, without closing into reverse intersections.

OFFSET CROSS Geometry

Crosses centered away from the primary 0°/180° intersections, creating split-cross, box-in-box, or multi-point secondary diamond fields.
